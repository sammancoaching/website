{{author.title}} is a technical coach and creator of [Augmented Coding Patterns]({{author.url}}), a catalog of patterns for coding effectively with AI agents, now grown with several co-authors. Her work explores what software craftsmanship becomes when agents write the code: how to embrace what these tools make possible while keeping the values and judgment behind good engineering, and how its practices need to evolve. She is a member of the Samman Technical Coaching Society.
